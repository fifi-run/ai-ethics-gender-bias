# Project scope and academic positioning
This repository contains a critical, literature-based coursework essay on the over-sexualisation of women in AI-generated imagery. It is a writing and argumentation sample, not an empirical machine-learning study.
When presenting this work, distinguish the essay's discussion of prior research from original empirical findings. It does not introduce a new dataset, train a model or conduct a fairness benchmark. Its themes include dataset bias, gender representation, platform and institutional incentives, algorithmic discrimination, and the limits of purely technical remedies.
The source PDF was supplied in the author's archive. Third-party illustrative images from the coursework PDF are not reproduced here.
